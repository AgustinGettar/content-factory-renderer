cannot supply prompt, model, input, payload or source URL. No historical
quote or fixed diagnostic operation is read to authorize this request.

The quote timestamp, USD estimate, exact payload SHA and applicable validity
are checkpointed. The validity is the earliest of sixty seconds from the
quote, provider expiry if returned, source expiry and verified account expiry.
Only then does ControlledV2Authorization issue a capability binding the human
confirmation, exact quote/payload and verified full wallet exposure.
The same payload bytes, credential fingerprint, signed evidence, quote,
authorization, accumulated budget and durable reservation are checked again
at the journal-protected generation boundary. Signed URLs stay in memory;
only their payload hash/expiry are checkpointed. No automatic quote renewal.

Estimated cost, reserved cost and actual cost are distinct. Actual cost stays
null until proven; reservations are not released on success, human review or
an ambiguous charge. The existing gate reserves the whole verified wallet
(up to the already authorized USD 4.64), within the USD 5 episode budget after
all recorded spend/reservations. The estimate is never a guaranteed charge
maximum. Exactly-once here means at most one local provider submission; a
lost response cannot prove provider execution, so it blocks resubmission.
