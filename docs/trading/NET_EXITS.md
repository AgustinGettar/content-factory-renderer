# Net exits and owner controls (PAPER only)

Schema 9 requires the isolated SQLite worker and the opt-in `LUMI_TRADING_NET_EXITS=net-exit-1.0.0`. No exchange order or funding endpoint is used. Existing secondary positions adopt their frozen expected net USD goal as a recorded management override. Their entry, quantity, stop, cash and original plan remain unchanged. New secondary trials begin with a separate prospective exit protocol after all old exposure closes.

Net P&L uses actual filled quantity, bid for long exits / ask for shorts, adverse slippage, actual entry costs, taker exit fee and elapsed funding periods. The same decimal calculator drives the snapshot and settlement. A last-trade target alone cannot close a managed position. Profit-taking requires sufficient observed BBO quantity, shared across same-account positions and repeated quotes; this is an all-or-none 10% participation approximation, not full order-book execution. Stops, liquidation and timeouts retain protective priority and can lose money. Candle highs during a gap cannot prove a net fill.

The private Site pins browser controls to the owner's authenticated identity and exact Origin. An R2 conditional-write queue records UUID requests, immutable operation ID/hash, ledger, creation and two-minute expiry. Only CLOSE (PROFIT or ANY) and positive tightening of the net goal are accepted. Neither route accepts an arbitrary market, destination, script, balance, leverage or exchange key.

AWS polls the authenticated queue every three seconds. Only the fenced engine writer applies requests. CLOSE waits at least one second and for a subsequent fresh quote; PROFIT rechecks positive net profit. Request receipts and financial effects commit together. Acknowledgements are read from committed SQLite, survive restart, and cannot cause another close on retry. Disconnection, expiry or insufficient liquidity never produces a false successful close. Manual/overridden outcomes remain in cash/history, are excluded from predictor training, and disqualify affected strategy validation.

The chart defaults to the visible candle range, with offscreen plan-level indicators and an explicit whole-plan scale. The dashboard publishes every 15 seconds and browser refreshes every five; the estimate is labelled, stale controls disable, and the worker rechecks at execution. The original expected goal is not lowered automatically. Users can choose a smaller positive net goal or manually close from the owner-only panel.

Verification: decimal long/short accounting including funding, stale data, latency, duplicate/expired requests, shared liquidity, stop precedence, gap handling, training exclusion, persisted receipts/restart, owner/CSRF rejection, R2 CAS retries and chart scale. Deployment script backs up SQLite after stopping the writer, fingerprints unchanged state, pins the release/scope, preserves environment secrets and checks stable service plus market/persistence/Telegram/backup health. No live paper position is manually closed for QA.
