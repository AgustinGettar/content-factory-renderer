## Sources and costs

Freqtrade's lookahead analysis, Hummingbot's independent controllers and price/time exits, and Jesse's robustness guidance informed the design. No claim is made that using these tools guarantees returns. The comparison uses actual Kraken Futures public data, two taker fees at 0.05% per side, 2 bps modeled slippage per side, observed bid/ask and signed hourly funding. It does not relabel spot data, assume maker fills or migrate the account to Binance. Kraken Futures and Binance USDT-margined regular-user base schedules both list 0.02% maker / 0.05% taker; eligibility, promotions and account-specific fees remain separate questions.
