# Operational secondary PAPER — 2026-11-01

Opt-in flag: LUMI_TRADING_SECONDARY_EXECUTION=secondary-futures-1.0.0. Only staging/PAPER/SQLITE. Runs in the existing worker and uses the existing frequency.account cash ledger. It does not add funds, restart the rejected trial or sum the three shadow mirrors. Historical ledgers, losses, learning and trial evidence are preserved. The old shadow comparator is not run concurrently when the operational flag is set. Removing the flag after migration fails closed.

Three closed-candle 5m hypotheses with closed 15m context: breakout retest, trend pullback, range reentry. A signal schedules a passive limit at a previously observed structural level rather than assuming a market fill at the confirmation close. Targets remain structural/short-horizon, never enlarged to pass the cost filter. Net RR remains at least 1.5. The entry expires after 120 seconds; positions have a 30-minute maximum holding request, subject to fresh executable liquidity. Pending orders are NOT represented as fills.

Real public PF_XBTUSD ticker and trade observations, contract metadata and signed funding. Simulated maker fills require an opposite-side subsequent trade strictly through the limit, after posting, with at least 20 times the requested quantity. Snapshot or duplicate trades cannot fill. Maker fee 0.02%; exits are taker 0.05% plus 2 bps modeled slippage, observed spread and accrued funding. This is not an actual exchange account and queue position is not modeled.

Shared risk: lesser of 0.5% equity and 2 USD per entry, 1% aggregate reserved loss, 3% daily loss budget, 5% new-execution drawdown stop, 10% cash reserve, at most 2x gross equity and two same-direction slots. Opposing BTC exposures and duplicate pattern positions are rejected. Three consecutive losses impose 15 minutes cooldown. Lifetime drawdown and previous losses remain in the account; the new execution baseline is explicitly recorded, not a new deposit. Gaps or slippage can exceed planned stop loss.

Entries and closures append the normal SELECTED/FILLED/CLOSED events with FREQUENT_PAPER ledger, update the original secondary history and counts, and queue private durable Telegram notices. The normal private projection displays the operational status, current futures market, decisions and positions; the previously rejected trial remains available as historical evidence. Manual net-goal and close controls use the position's futures quote, never the primary spot quote.

Tests distinguish software correctness from profitability. They cover flat/idempotent activation, conserved cash and net P&L, shared reservations, causal closed candles, real-mode rejection, maker fill evidence, duplicate events, gaps, funding, manual controls, notices and projection. No claim of a validated trading edge is made. Historical OHLC data cannot establish actual maker fills. Future results must be measured net of all costs without erasing losses.
